🌱 About Our Project

Smart Agriculture Management System is a web-based project designed to help farmers organize important farming information in one place. It provides pages for crop management, soil information, irrigation planning, and plant disease detection.

The system is intended to make agricultural information easier to manage and understand. Farmers can enter field details and view information or recommendations based on the values they provide.

This project is a learning and demonstration system. Soil readings and field conditions must be entered by the user or supplied by suitable sensors or connected services.

🌾 Main Features

Crop Management

Record crop names, field area, sowing dates, harvest dates, and crop status.

Soil Management

Enter soil type, pH, nutrient values, and moisture information.

Irrigation Management

Record water levels, soil moisture, and irrigation methods.

Disease Detection

Provide a place to add plant disease detection features or connect an AI model.

Dashboard

Display a summary of agricultural records and entered information.

User Access

Login and Sign In pages can be used as the starting point for user accounts.
